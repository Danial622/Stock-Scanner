Strategy & Scoring Methodology

1 Technical Score (0-100)

  • Trend (35 pts): Price > EMA20 (+10), Price > EMA50 (+10), Price > EMA200 (+8), Golden Cross (+7).
  • RSI (15 pts): 50–65 sweet spot (+15), 65–70 (+8), 40–50 (+5). Overbought >75 (-8).
  • MACD (15 pts): Bullish histogram & line above zero (+15).
  • Bollinger (10 pts): Price moving between mid and upper band (+10).
  • Liquidity (10 pts): Daily traded value ≥ 30B Toman (+10).
  • Momentum (15 pts): Daily change 1–4% (+15). Over-extended >12% above EMA20 (-10).

2 Smart Money Flow (±10 pts)

Analyzes real (حقیقی) vs institutional (حقوقی) volumes. If institutions are net buyers, the score gets a bonus. If they are heavily selling, points are deducted.

3 Fundamentals (Codal + TSETMC)

A bonus/penalty of up to ±20 points based on:

  • Valuation: Forward P/E vs Sector P/E. Cheap = bonus. Expensive = penalty.
  • Earnings: Forecast EPS growth > 20% = bonus. Loss-making (EPS ≤ 0) = -25 points and capped at "Watchlist".
  • Codal Events: Dividends (+4), Capital increase from reserves (+4), Cash capital increase (-4).
  • Red Flags: Trading halt (-10), Qualified audit opinion (-20).

4 Trading Levels (ATR-Based)

  • Stop Loss: Entry - 1.5 × ATR (Volatility adjusted).
  • T1 (Safe): Entry + 1.5 × risk. Sell 50% here.
  • T2 (Main): Entry + 2.5 × risk.
  • T3 (Runner): Entry + 4.0 × risk.
